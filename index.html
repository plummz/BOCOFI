How to test

  1. Open the kiosk and the app in two tabs on the same browser.
  2. Log in to the app using juandelacruz@gmail.com and PIN 1234.
  3. On the kiosk, tap Recycle Today, then Start, then Log in.
  4. In the app, go to Recycle and type the 4-letter code shown on the kiosk.
  5. Use the item buttons on the kiosk to insert bottles or cans, then choose your reward.
